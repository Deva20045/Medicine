## Source discrepancies and clinical-safety handling

The book remains the coverage source, but questionable wording is **not silently
converted into clinical fact**. Explanations distinguish the printed statement from
a clarification. This is educational material, not an individual treatment protocol.

- **p883:** Raw QT “>12 small boxes” is contextualized with ECG speed and QTc.
- **p885:** Hungry-bone PTH cell is blank; no value is invented. The graph's operative
  arrow/time-zero placement is inconsistent, so exact operative timing is not inferred.
  PPHP's “no change” cell is not taught as absent PTH responsiveness. Gsα is a signaling
  protein, not a receptor.
- **p886:** The 2–3-minute calcium-gluconate line is separated from monitored guidance;
  salt mass is not elemental calcium. Clinical guidance supports 10–20 mL of 10%
  calcium gluconate over about 10 minutes with ECG/calcium monitoring.
  [2](https://handbook.ggcmedicines.org.uk/guidelines/electrolyte-disturbances/management-of-hypocalcaemia/)
  [3](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/6264/smpc)
- **p887–888:** Calbindin is not vitamin-D-binding protein or a phosphate transporter;
  the fixed “99% PCT” statement is not used as a universal physiological constant.
  Fanconi syndrome is distinguished from isolated proximal RTA. Klotho resistance
  is impaired FGF23 effectiveness, not necessarily low circulating FGF23. Vascular
  calcification is not an automatic universal transplant exclusion.
- **p890–891:** Mild-depletion “no treatment” versus oral-replacement entries are both
  disclosed. The reversed FGF23 arrow is corrected mechanistically; PHEX/DMP1 action
  is not reduced to direct proteolysis. Tumor-induced osteomalacia does not require
  a germline FGF23 gain-of-function mutation. The 2 g dietary figure is source-specific.
- **p892–894:** “Hypomagnesuria” is not silently relabeled as hypomagnesemia.
  Claudin-16/19 disease is associated with hypercalciuria/nephrocalcinosis. The printed
  >2 mEq/L arrest entry is flagged rather than repaired to an invented number; Merck
  describes arrest at much higher concentrations in severe toxicity.
  [3](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/nephrology/electrolyte-disorders/hypermagnesemia)
  The MgSO4 “1–2 mg” loading entry is flagged as a unit error, not endorsed as a dose;
  adult emergency regimens use gram quantities and differ by clinical context.
  [1](https://www.proacls.com/training/video/magnesium-sulfate)
- **p895:** Lithium's “hypoparathyroidism” wording is corrected to its relevant
  hyperparathyroid/CaSR effect. Source population/fracture rankings are not universal
  prevalence claims. Drug risks are not claims that each exposure invariably causes harm.
- **p896:** Z-scores are not obsolete: they remain preferred in premenopausal women
  and men under 50. [2](https://iscd.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/ISCD2007OfficialPositions-Adult.pdf)
  T-score ≤−2.5 includes the boundary. FRAX is a calculated risk, not directly measured
  by DXA; the source's wording is qualified.
- **p897:** **150 mg ibandronate monthly is oral**, while the usual IV regimen is
  **3 mg every three months**—not the source's 150 mg IV monthly.
  [1](https://www.drugs.com/dosage/ibandronate.html)
  [3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/getFile.cfm?setid=a70566e1-cbe5-472a-85ae-08a14117552a&name=IBANDRONATE+SODIUM)
  Drug-specific renal thresholds, abaloparatide's PTHrP identity, risk-based treatment
  selection and non-routine/investigational drug status are distinguished from the
  simplified hierarchy. Raloxifene's broad “agonist everywhere” wording does not
  override its thromboembolic risk.
- **p898–899:** Congenital is not synonymous with Mendelian inheritance. The duct
  timeline and classical C-cell lineage are qualified. Basal hormone synthesis does
  not exclude central hypothyroidism. TTF2 cleft wording and dysgenesis gene-frequency
  claims are not accepted literally.
- **p900:** Organification is TPO-dependent; a cold nodule is not automatically
  malignant; thiocyanate/perchlorate pharmacology is not a recommendation for routine
  treatment of all amiodarone thyrotoxicosis. The printed T4:T3 ratios are explicitly
  assigned to their diagram locations rather than treated as serum free-hormone ratios.
- **p901:** Urinary iodine amount and concentration are not interchangeable. Isotope
  therapy is distinguished from diagnostic scanning. The “Pathway 4:1” row is labeled
  as an interpretation of potency. The three-stage illness chart is source-specific,
  not a mandatory course, and clinically indicated thyroid testing remains appropriate.
